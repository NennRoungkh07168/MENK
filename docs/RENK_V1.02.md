# RENK v1.02

## Much bigger offline research pack
- app/offline_topics.txt — 120 medicine topics: everything including FDA records,
  side effects and prescribing information
- app/study_topics.txt — about 180 study topics, research only (no FDA): how the body works,
  pharmacology, diseases and public health (incl. tropical diseases common in Cambodia and
  South-East Asia), nutrition and vegetables, herbs, traditional medicine and extraction
- For every topic: research counts, 25 most relevant papers with abstracts,
  25 open-access review articles to study, and clinical trials
- Expect the app to grow to roughly 100–200 MB with the pack inside
- Build or refresh it:  bash termux/build-pack.sh  (about 2–4 hours on GitHub)

## Papers tab: "Review articles to study"
Switch between the most relevant papers and open-access review articles, which summarise
a whole topic and can be read free in full.

## New library sections (offline)
- Vegetables & foods (10): broccoli family (sulforaphane), tomato (lycopene), leafy greens,
  carrot and sweet potato (vitamin A), beetroot (nitrate), onion (quercetin), legumes,
  bitter melon, moringa, grapefruit (food–medicine interaction)
- Herb & plant extraction (8): teas (infusion, decoction), tinctures, oil infusions,
  steam distillation and essential oils, industrial solvent and supercritical CO₂,
  standardised extracts and quality, how labs identify compounds, solvent polarity
Library total: 128 entries.
