# MENK v0.7

## Side effects and prescribing information (Medicine tab)
- Side effects from the official label, plus the 15 most-reported side effects in the
  FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS), with a clear note that reports are not proof
- Official prescribing information: dosage and administration, pregnancy and specific
  groups, shown as label text for prescribers with a warning not to self-dose
- Guides: How prescriptions work · Understanding side effects · Dose basics

## PDF reports
- Save any search as a PDF (button at the top right of results): evidence counts,
  papers, trials with results, approvals, label sections, side effects, manufacturers,
  recalls and chemistry
- Save any library page as a PDF
- Uses Android's built-in PdfDocument; you choose where to save, no permissions needed

## Offline
- Library (109 entries) is built in and always works offline
- Every search is saved on the device; searching the same topic offline shows the saved copy,
  with an "Offline" notice. Structure images are saved too.

## Research outside the US
- Papers: filter by the authors' country (Europe PMC affiliation search)
- Trials: filter by study-site country (ClinicalTrials.gov location search); trial cards list countries
- Library sections with a country filter:
  research institutes & labs, universities, hospitals & clinics, regulators & health systems,
  pharma companies — Germany, India, China, Russia, Spain, Cambodia, EU, US and WHO
- Chinese herbs: sweet wormwood (artemisinin), ginseng, astragalus, licorice, ephedra

## Limits
Regulators outside the US (EMA, CDSCO, NMPA, Russia's GRLS, Cambodia's DDF) don't offer
open data connections like openFDA, so their information is in the library, not live.
