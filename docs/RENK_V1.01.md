# RENK v1.01 — Research Evidence Network of Knowledge

Renamed from MENK. Same gold logo launcher icon. Installs over the previous app.

## New
- Evidence tab = Evidence PDF library: every PDF you save is stored on the device,
  listed with date and size, opens inside the app without internet (Android PdfRenderer),
  can be copied to Downloads ("Save a copy") or deleted
- PDF button on every search and every library page saves straight into that library
- Brand names work in Chemistry: "Adderall" is resolved through its US label to its
  active ingredients (dextroamphetamine, amphetamine), each with structure and properties
- Brand names also work for US products/manufacturers and FDA side-effect reports
- New papers not yet tagged by PubMed get an estimated study level from the title, marked *
- New guide: Good prescribing — the WHO 6 steps
- Fixed two compile errors in the v0.7 offline-cache code

## Kept from v0.7 (first build of these features)
Side effects (label + FDA reports), official prescribing information, offline saved searches,
country filters for papers and trials, 110 library entries across Germany, India, China,
Russia, Spain, Cambodia, EU, US and WHO.
